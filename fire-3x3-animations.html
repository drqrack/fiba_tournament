FIRE 3x3 – Animation Kit

Each block below is self-contained. Copy the CSS (in <style>), the HTML, and the matching JS into your page.

1. Spinning basketball

Good beside the event title in the header. Change the size with style="--size:64px".

2. Player shooting – swish!

Loops forever. Works well in the hero section above the payment box.

3. Bouncing-ball loader on the Submit button

Tap the button to see it. In your form code, call setButtonLoading(btn, true) before sending and false after.

4. Ghana-colours confetti on the success screen

Call fireConfetti() when the registration is saved.

5. Scroll reveal

Add class="reveal" to any section and it fades up as it scrolls into view. Every card on this page uses it.
